# AlertsUkraine
AlertsUkraine — a small, always-on-top desktop Rainmeter widget (skin) for Windows that shows the current air-raid alert status for up to 5 Ukrainian regions of your choice.

! Disclaimer: This widget is informational only. Do not rely on it for safety decisions. Always follow official alert channels.

Features:
- Live alert status per region: ALERT (red) or Clear (green)
- Choose up to 5 regions out of 26 in a built-in Settings window
- Your selection is saved and restored after restarting Rainmeter
- Click the title to open the full web version of the alert map
- Two variants of skin included. You can choose between two or five regions at a time

Installation:
1. Install Rainmeter
2. Copy the files into your Rainmeter folder (..\Rainmeter\Skins\)
3. Open Rainmeter and click Manage option. From the list of available skins choose AlertsUkraine.ini and click Load
4. If nothing appears try clicking Refresh all button

Alert data is provided by https://ubilling.net.ua/aerialalerts/
